## Principles

1. **Everything is written down.** Amounts owed, payments, advances, refunds and write-offs are entries in a book.
2. **Two sides.** The person who records an entry can never be the one who confirms it. An amount counts as settled only when the other person confirms it.
3. **Your point of view.** The same entry is shown to each person in their own words.
4. **Nothing is guessed.** The system never invents an amount from a contract or a conversation.

## Books

* **Personal book.** Your own private income and expenses.
* **Shared books.** Between people who work together.
* **Project books.** For the money of one piece of work.

## Money inside a deal

When an agreement has rates, work reported in the deal produces an amount **earned**. The Money view then shows, depending on your role:

* **If you are owed:** what I am owed, what I have received, and what remains owed.
* **If you owe:** what I owe, what I have paid, and what remains to pay.

An **advance** paid before work starts is kept as credit and applied to what is earned later. If more was paid than was earned, the difference can be returned or carried forward. A person who has done work can **ask** for a share of what is owed, for example half, and the other side sees and confirms it.

## Paying in goods or services

A settlement may be made **in kind**: goods or services handed over instead of money, at a value both sides agreed. It is recorded as a settlement of the debt and confirmed by the other side like any payment. See "[Barter, in-kind settlement and inflation](/docs/barter)" for what we still have to work out.

## Units

Amounts are kept in the unit you choose, such as toman. Different units are never silently mixed or converted.

## Corrections

Entries are not edited away. A mistake is corrected by a refund, a write-off or an adjustment that stays visible.

## What is not here yet

* A single statement per person across all the deals they are part of.
* Project expenses with reimbursement.
* Platform fees: **none are charged today.** If we ever add one, it will be announced openly and explained in these pages first.
