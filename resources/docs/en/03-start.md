## Joining

New accounts are created from a private invitation link. Someone who already uses the system, or the administrators, can send you one. Open the link, create your account, and confirm your email address.

If you only want to read these pages or send us a proposal, you do not need an account.

## Your profile

Your profile is how other people recognise you: your name, a short description and a picture. You decide what is visible. Skills, needs and offers are recorded where they belong, not hidden in the profile.

## Today: your starting point

After you sign in, **Today** shows what needs you first:

* what is **waiting for you**: a reply, an approval, a report to review;
* what you are **waiting on others for**;
* the activities planned for today;
* your active needs, offers and deals;
* a short summary of your money for the day.

Today only summarises. Each item takes you to the real place where you act on it, and nothing on Today creates or changes anything by itself.

## The main areas

| Area | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Needs & offers | Say what you need or can offer, and find what fits |
| Work (Deals) | Follow each agreement from first contact to settlement |
| Planner | Plan your own days, habits and things to do later |
| Money | See what you are owed, what you owe and what has been settled |
| Content | Write, read and share documents, lessons and media |
| Businesses & groups | Work as part of an organisation or a community |
| Messages | Talk to people you work with |
| Help | These pages |

## Language, calendar and appearance

The system works in English and Persian (with more languages to come), right-to-left and left-to-right, in light and dark appearance, and on phones as well as computers. Persian users can see dates in the Persian calendar with the Gregorian date available alongside. Your choices are remembered.
