## Finished and in use

Needs and offers with matching; deals with versioned agreements, work reports and a money book; the planner with habits and a "for later" list; content with media, crop and camera; contacts, messages, groups and businesses; Persian and English with right-to-left and dark appearance.

## The next steps toward a complete baseline

1. **These pages and the invitation to collaborate.**
2. **Checking and aligning** what has been released, and tidying the code base.
3. **Subcontracting.** A contractor employs others under a project, with each agreement private to its two sides and an overview for the contractor.
4. **Money for projects.** Project expenses and reimbursement, refunds and unused credit, settlement in kind with an agreed value, and one statement per person.
5. **Foundations that do nothing yet.** A place for a future platform fee, set to zero, and a record of who invited whom, without paying anything.
6. **A complete walkthrough** from invitation to settlement in both languages, on phone and computer.

## After that

* A catalogue for businesses and a public way to ask them questions.
* Verified track records people may choose to show.
* A careful, public design of any fee, of any reward for bringing in people who then do real work, of valuing goods and services against each other, and of whether any internal unit is needed. Nothing in this group is switched on without review.

## How we work

We build in small steps, test each one, and keep every change reversible. We would rather ship something modest that is right than something large that is wrong.
