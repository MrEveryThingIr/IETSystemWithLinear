## One simple idea

A **need** is something you are looking for. An **offer** is something you can provide. Both are recorded the same way: a guided form asks what it is, in what quantity, where, when, on what terms, and who may see it.

## Recording one

1. Open **Needs & offers** and choose to record a need or an offer.
2. Pick what kind of thing it is: a service, goods, property, capital, a collaboration, and so on. The form then asks only the questions that make sense for that kind.
3. Describe it in your own words and add photos if they help.
4. Choose who may see it.
5. Review and record it.

You can edit it later. Earlier versions are kept so that anyone who responded can see what they responded to.

## Visibility

You choose whether an item is private, visible to people you work with, or public. Nothing is shown more widely than you chose.

## Matches

For any of your active needs or offers, the system can show **possible matches**: other people's items that seem compatible. Each match explains *why* it fits, and respects privacy, so you only see what you are allowed to see.

A match is only a suggestion. It creates nothing and tells the other person nothing until you decide to respond.

## Responding

When you respond to a need or an offer, a **deal** begins. The next chapter explains what happens then.
