## Contacts and messages

Adding someone as a **contact** lets you share more with them and see what they chose to share with contacts. **Messages** are private conversations, with files and photos. A message never creates an agreement; only the accept step in a deal does.

## Groups

A group is a community: members, roles and permissions, shared spaces and shared agreements. Joining is by invitation. What a person may do inside a group comes from their role there and nowhere else.

## Businesses

A business has its own page, members, job titles and roles, and can describe what it offers. A member may act for the business in deals if they have been authorised, and the record shows who acted.

## Employment and chains of work (being built)

We are working on the natural next step: a contractor who takes a job and **employs other people under it**, buys materials and pays expenses for that project. The design keeps each agreement between two parties, and links them: the client's agreement with the contractor, and the contractor's agreement with each worker. Each side sees only its own agreement; a contractor's margin stays private. This is *partly built* and is the next major piece of work.

## One place for related things

Deals, businesses and groups overlap in real life. We are looking for a way to treat them as variations of a shared "space" with the same membership and roles, without losing what makes each useful.
