## Trust comes from rules, not promises

The system does not ask you to trust anyone's word. It makes it hard to do the wrong thing quietly.

* **Exact acceptance.** You accept a specific version of a proposal or agreement, never "whatever it is now".
* **History is kept.** Changes make new versions. Nothing is silently edited or deleted.
* **Two sides for money.** One person records; the other confirms.
* **Evidence.** Work reports can carry photos and files; reviews refer to them.
* **Words are not actions.** Writing "approved" or "paid" in a message changes nothing. Only the matching step does.

## Privacy

* Things are private until you share them.
* You choose the audience of your needs, offers and content.
* Matching and search only show what you are allowed to see.
* Planner links and notes never give others access to your documents.
* Proposals sent through these pages are used only to answer you.

## Honest limits

* A record shows what people said and confirmed. It cannot prove that work was good or that a photo is genuine.
* A determined person may still try to cheat. We want help finding the ways, so we can close them.
* Parts of this software were written with AI assistance. A page in the product explains how it was developed, and people review the result.

## What we are researching

Which verified facts should become part of a person's visible track record, with their consent, without turning into a score that can be gamed or used unfairly. This is deliberately separate from reviews and ratings.
