## Who can take part

Anyone. A retired accountant, a site foreman, a student of economics, a shopkeeper, a lawyer, a programmer, a teacher, a person who simply lives with unstable prices. You do not need an account and you do not need a title.

## Ways to help

* **Review a chapter** from your professional point of view and tell us what is wrong or missing.
* **Propose a design** for a rule, a term, a screen or a calculation.
* **Share a case**: something that happened in your work that the system should handle.
* **Join a pilot** with a small group of real people.
* **Help translate** these pages into another language, or improve our Persian and English.
* **Build**: if you write software, tell us what you could contribute.

## What to include

The [form](/docs/collaborate) asks about your field, expertise and education so we can read your proposal in context. It does not require any particular qualification. Then write:

1. the problem, in one or two sentences;
2. what you suggest;
3. why you think it would work, ideally with an example from real life;
4. what could go wrong.

A short, concrete proposal is worth more than a long essay.

## What happens next

We read every proposal and write back. Some will change a chapter or the design. Some we will disagree with, and we will say why. If you allow it, we credit your name where your idea is used. Your details are used only to answer you.

## A promise

We will not claim your idea as ours without credit, will not sell your information, and will not ask you for money.
