## Content is for people to read and learn from

Content is where you write and share: guides, lessons, notes, specifications, a portfolio. It has an editor (the **Studio**) and a clean **Reader**.

## Who can read it

When you finish a piece you choose its audience:

* **Only me.**
* **My contacts.** The people you have added as contacts.
* **Public.** Anyone with the link, and it can appear in the public library.

Drafts are visible only to you. Publishing creates an edition that does not change afterwards; further changes make a new edition, so a reference to a particular edition always shows what it showed.

## Pictures, video and sound

You can add images from your gallery, or take a photo or record a video straight from your phone. Before an image is uploaded you can **crop** it. You can add several images at once. Each image can have a caption, and, where the author asks a question, readers can answer about a specific image.

## Reading together

Readers can highlight a passage and attach a note, a question or a correction to that exact place. The author sees them next to the text.

## Linked to your plans

A plan or a day in the planner can link to your notes. This only points to the document; it never changes who can read it.

## Not the same as these pages

These documentation pages are separate from Content. They are part of the product, they are not written by users, and they never appear in the public library.
